Data Quality for Responsible AI in Energy Systems

DARES investigates the relationship between energy systems, data and AI, focusing on data quality for responsible AI in the green transition. As AI supports ambitious emissions goals, poor-quality data can undermine decisions and create risks for energy infrastructure.

Current data quality definitions remain vague, and systematic research into assessment is limited. DARES brings together an interdisciplinary Nordic-Baltic community to build regional expertise and advance socio-technical approaches to data quality.
